EVERY SHOP IS ITS OWN COMMUNITY

One marketplace. Many little worlds.

On V2V, each seller is more than a list of products. Every shop has its own marketplace of pieces and its own community feed, so collectors can come back to the people they trust and see what they are working on.

JarFriends is one of them: its own collection, its own drops and its own posts, inside V2V. Your shop works the same way.

  • All of it reaches everyone browsing the V2V market
